01 / THE APPROACH

Good clips start with judgment.

The goal isn’t to cut a long video into arbitrary pieces. It’s to spot a complete thought, give it a strong opening, and keep the creator’s meaning intact.

01

Find the point

Review the episode for useful, surprising, or memorable moments that can stand without the full conversation.

02

Shape the story

Cut for clarity and pace. Add readable captions, a purposeful opening, and framing that feels like your brand.

03

Make it publishable

Deliver vertical files and concise context so you can review, approve, and share with less work on your side.
